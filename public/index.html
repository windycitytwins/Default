Cheat sheet

  • Trend — higher highs+lows = up; lower = down.
  • Support/Resistance — floors & ceilings; watch for breaks & bounces.
  • MA — direction & dynamic support; crossovers signal momentum shifts.
  • Volume — confirms conviction behind a move.
  • RSI — >70 overbought, <30 oversold.
